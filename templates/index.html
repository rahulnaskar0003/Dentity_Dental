Our Services

All Your Dental Needs at One Place

Root Canal, Tooth Filling and Emergency Dental Treatment

Laser RCT, Painless Root Canal Treatment, Re-RCT, Microscopic RCT, Rotary Endodontics and Single Sitting RCT always helps to save infected teeth. The Tooth-Coloured Fillings treat cavities, while Emergency Dental Treatment provides immediate care for severe tooth pain(If Pulpally involved by Emergency Pulpectomy) Immediate care of dental infection, swelling, broken teeth and dental trauma.

Dental Implants, Crowns, Bridges and Dentures

Dental Implants, including Single, Multiple, All-on-4, All-on-6, Immediate and Basal Implants, replace missing teeth. Bone grafting and Sinus Lift procedures are performed when required. Zirconia Crowns, Ceramic Crowns, Dental Bridges, including Complete, Partial, Flexible and Implant Supported Dentures help to restore beautiful smiles.

Dental Braces, Clear Aligners and Smile Makeup Treatment

Orthodontic Treatment with Metallic, Ceramic and Self-Ligating Dental Braces helps to correct zigzag teeth, including crowding, spacing and bite problems. Clear Aligners, 3D Oral Scanning, Invisible Braces, Digital Smile Designing and Porcelain Veneers help to improve teeth alignment, shape and provides beautiful, natural smile.

Teeth Cleaning, Gum Treatment and Teeth Whitening

Ultrasonic Scaling, Teeth Cleaning, deep Gum Cleaning, and Polishing remove hard plaque, tartar and stains. The Gum Disease Treatment, Electrosurgery, Laser Gum therapy, Crown Lengthening and Gum Grafting helps to improve periodontal health and condition. The Teeth Whitening and Laser Teeth Bleaching brighten the hard stained teeth.

Tooth Extraction, Wisdom Tooth Removal and Oral Surgery

Painless Tooth Extraction and Impacted Wisdom Tooth Removal Surgery can be done using minimally invasive and Piezo Surgical Techniques for speedy recovery. The Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery includes Oral Biopsy, Cyst Removal Treatment, Jaw Surgery, Fracture Management, TMJ Treatment and Oral Lesion Treatment is required for complex conditions.

Children's, Preventive and Advanced Dental Care

Children's Dental Treatment includes Cavity Treatment, Fluoride Therapy, Space Maintainers, and Dental Sealants are used to protect growing teeth. The Preventive Dentistry helps to improve long term oral health. Advanced technologies including RVG, Apex Locator, Dental Loupes, 3D Oral Scanners, Needless Injection and Conscious Sedation are necessary to enhance dental care.
